# Forge Tracker
## The FitNotes App Gym Companion

The Forge Tracker is a HTML companion to the workout tracking app FitNotes, the goal is to add gamification and gratification to working out to encourage people to reach their goals.
Forge is currently in very early stages, has gone through many major changes in a short time and will continue to do so.

## Installation

No installation needed, everything runs directly within the webpage, no data leaves your device.

## Usage

To access the tracker, follow the link here to [The Forge Tracker](https://AlscarB.github.io/forge-tracker/)
